Who We Are

Agricultural Supplies
You Can Trust

Irio Seeds & AgroChem is a dedicated agricultural distributor based at Phakalane Industrial, Gaborone. We supply a comprehensive range of crop inputs — from certified seeds to crop protection chemicals — to support farmers and agribusinesses throughout Botswana.

Our goal is to make quality agricultural products accessible and affordable, backed by knowledgeable service that helps our customers get the most out of every growing season.
